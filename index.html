Privacy Policy

Totem · Last updated 15 August 2026

Totem does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

What is stored

Your alarms (times, labels, and repeat days) and an identifier for the NFC tag you register are saved only on your device. The number of emergency overrides you have used is stored in the device keychain so that it survives reinstalling the app.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Totem has no user accounts, no backend server, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or advertising code. No data is ever uploaded.

Permissions

Notifications are used only to sound your alarms. NFC is used only to read the identifier of the tag you register, so the app can recognise that same tag later. Face ID or your device passcode is used only to confirm an emergency override; the result of that check is never stored or shared, and Totem never receives your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes your alarms and your registered tag. The emergency override count intentionally persists in the device keychain, so that the monthly limit cannot be bypassed by reinstalling the app. That value is a small number of timestamps and contains no personal information.

Children

Totem is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date above.

Contact
